Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RetroSoul Games handles information in connection with the mobile game Cedar Shadows: Hana.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Overview

Cedar Shadows: Hana is a mobile game developed and published by RetroSoul Games.

The game does not require users to create an account or sign in in order to play.

2. Information We Collect

RetroSoul Games does not require players to provide personal information such as their name, postal address, phone number, or account credentials in order to play Cedar Shadows: Hana.

The game uses third-party advertising services. These services may automatically collect or process certain device and usage information as described below.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Cedar Shadows: Hana uses Google AdMob, provided by Google, to display advertisements. The game may display interstitial advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • app and advertisement interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information; and
  • other information necessary for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

The way Google processes information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

4. Rewarded Advertisements

Cedar Shadows: Hana may offer rewarded advertisements in specific parts of the game. Watching a rewarded advertisement is initiated by the player in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as continuing gameplay.

5. Local Game Data

The game may store gameplay information locally on the user's device, such as game progress, settings, or high scores.

Local game data is used to provide game functionality and may be removed when the application is uninstalled or its local storage is cleared.

6. How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party services may be used for purposes including:

  • displaying and delivering advertisements;
  • measuring advertising performance;
  • analytics and service improvement;
  • diagnostics and performance monitoring; and
  • fraud prevention and security.

7. Data Sharing

RetroSoul Games does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the game, including Google AdMob, in accordance with their respective privacy policies and for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

Cedar Shadows: Hana is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Advertising and privacy settings will be configured in accordance with the applicable platform requirements, advertising policies, and the intended audience of the game.

9. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with privacy or consent choices relating to advertising and the processing of their information.

Users may also have advertising and privacy controls available through their device settings and Google services.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the game. Information transmitted by third-party services is handled according to the security practices of those service providers.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Cedar Shadows: Hana does not currently provide user accounts. Gameplay information stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game.

Data processed by third-party services may be retained and deleted according to the policies and legal obligations of those service providers.

12. Third-Party Services

The game currently uses Google AdMob for advertising. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its services, or applicable legal and platform requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
